EK GARIB KI DUA LAGEGI AAPKO

Kya ye saare lectures aur notes bilkul free hain?

Haan, Emerging Sciences par uplabdh sabhi lectures aur study material 100% free hain. Iske liye koi fees ya subscription ki zarurat nahi hai.

Main apna doubt kaise pooch sakta hoon?

Aap apne doubts poochne ke liye upar diye gaye **Public doubt** (GitHub Giscus comments) ka use kar sakte hain ya fir seedha **Private doubt** ke liye Google Form bhar sakte hain. Sabse tez jawab ke liye aap hamare Telegram channel se bhi jud sakte hain.

Kya mujhe higher mathematics ya IIT JAM ke liye alag portal mil jayega?

Haan! Agar aap IIT JAM, CUET PG ya TIFR ki taiyari kar rahe hain, toh aap homepage par diye gaye link se hamare dedicated **Higher Education Portal** par ja sakte hain.

Naye lectures aur notes kab upload hote hain?

Naye chapters aur lectures syllabus ke anusar regular update kiye jaate hain. Jis chapter ke samne "Coming soon" likha hai, wahan jald hi content add kar diya jayega.
